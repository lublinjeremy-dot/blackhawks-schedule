Six Standards of Football
How we play — every rep, every play
Body — Fast, Physical, Relentless
Not based on outcome, stats, or talent. Based on character. Something anyone can always work towards.
Mind — Educated, Disciplined, Composed
Take the coaching given and study your craft. Know what you must do and do it. Regulate your emotions.
Play to the Standard, Not the Circumstance
How you play never changes. All the little things matter, every single time.
6 Seconds of Memory
One play at a time. Each play ends when it ends. We don’t worry about the past, we don’t think beyond right now, this play.
Repetition to Production
Create good habits on and off the field. Neural pathways. Muscle memory.
Process to Progress
Immerse yourself in the process. A desire to work and be on the journey. Crave the discomfort — it leads to growth.
None of this is about talent.
It’s effort. It’s mental control.
Always working to be better.
It’s identity.

Program Standards
Sprint through every line.
Touch every line with your hand.
Give full effort every rep.
During Blackhawk Shuttles, if one player misses a line, cuts a rep short, or quits before the whistle — the team returns to 0 reps.
The team finishes together.
If players are failing due to excessive fatigue, heat, or safety concerns, coaches should adjust volume, rest periods, or rep totals accordingly while maintaining effort standards.
